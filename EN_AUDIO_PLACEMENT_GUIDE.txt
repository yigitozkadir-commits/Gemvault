VOICE PROFILE REMINDER
======================================================================
- DEFNE voice: use the SAME ElevenLabs voice in both Ad 1 and Ad 2
  (for brand/character consistency).
- MOLO voice: only appears in Ad 2 — young/energetic, slightly high-
  pitched, comically exaggerated tone.
- Ad 2 Scenes 5 and 6: two-line scenes — record both voices separately
  and merge them into a single mp3 (Descript or Audacity, ~0.3-0.5s gap
  between lines).
- Numbers in English are spelled out ("seven hundred thirty-five",
  "eighteen") rather than written as digits — this gives cleaner TTS
  pronunciation than numerals.
